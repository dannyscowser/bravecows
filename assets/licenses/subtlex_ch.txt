SUBTLEX-CH Chinese Frequency Data

Character frequency information is derived from the SUBTLEX-CH corpus, developed by Cai, Q., and Brysbaert, M. (2010).

The dataset is based on word and character frequencies from Chinese film and television subtitles.

Source: https://www.ugent.be/pp/experimentele-psychologie/en/research/documents/subtlexch

Citation:
Cai, Q., & Brysbaert, M. (2010). SUBTLEX-CH: Chinese Word and Character Frequency Data from Film and TV Subtitles.
